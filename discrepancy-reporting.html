Data Visualization

Discrepancy Reporting Suite

Operations teams were finding discrepancies by scrolling through exported spreadsheets line by line. This case study covers how proportion and severity were made visible at a glance instead.

Role

UX/UI Designer

Client / Team

Ideagensys

Timeline

Sep 2023 – Nov 2025

Tools

Figma, Adobe XD

Share: LinkedIn X Email
>

The challenge

Reporting discrepancies were being tracked through flat tabular exports. Nothing in the layout communicated which discrepancies were large, small, or urgent - every row looked the same weight.

Operations teams needed a way to triage at a glance, not read every row before deciding where to look first.

Process

  • Mapped the existing information architecture to see where severity and volume data already existed but weren't being shown.
  • Prototyped a treemap view to encode proportion and a paired bar-chart view for direct comparison between categories.
  • Tested different opacity and color-weighting schemes with the operations team to find encodings that read correctly without a legend.

Solution

A treemap surfaced proportion and severity in one view, sized and shaded so the biggest problems were visually the biggest.

A bar-chart drill-down let the team compare specific categories directly once they'd identified where to look.

Results

Operations teams could identify where to focus without reading through full exports first.

The treemap/bar-chart pairing was adopted as the standard view for ongoing discrepancy reporting.
