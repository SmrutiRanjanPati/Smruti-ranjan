Enterprise UX · Complaint Management

Customer Care Portal

Manufacturing customers expect timely responses and documented proof that quality issues have been resolved, but the existing process ran on email, spreadsheets and manual follow-ups. This case study covers how five different roles were given one shared, structured complaint record instead.

The challenge

Complaints were tracked through emails, spreadsheets and manual follow-ups, so stakeholders had no shared view of the complaint lifecycle. Customers had limited visibility, while internal teams spent significant time searching for information and chasing owners for updates.

Five different roles - customer, complaint creator, coordinator, resolution owner and action owner - each needed a different view of the same complaint, with no centralized dashboard, unclear action ownership and no structured way to verify a fix actually held.

Process

  • Mapped the end-to-end complaint-resolution workflow, from first customer report through investigation, corrective action, QC review and effectiveness verification.
  • Defined role-based experiences and information architecture so five roles stayed connected to one shared complaint record instead of five separate views.
  • Designed the dashboard, complaint list, complaint detail, 8D report, action management and QC review experiences around that shared record.
  • Built interaction patterns for evidence upload, status tracking, assignment and approvals, translating the 8D quality methodology into a clear enterprise UX.

Solution

A role-based dashboard surfaces what needs attention next - open complaints, pending QC reviews, overdue actions and effectiveness checks - rather than just a count. The complaint detail screen acts as a single source of truth, combining complaint information, 8D progress, actions, evidence and the activity timeline in one place.

Complaint creation is progressive: customers submit basic details first, while the complaint creator adds the technical information needed for investigation. A structured 8D workflow (D0-D8) with Fishbone and 5 Why analysis keeps root-cause work consistent, and a dedicated action-management view gives every corrective or preventive action a clear owner, due date and evidence trail through to QC approval and customer closure.

Results

This is a concept case study - the following are proposed targets, not measured outcomes. Customers would be able to track complaints without repeated follow-up emails, and management would get real-time visibility into complaint volume, ownership and bottlenecks.

A standardized 8D workflow and dedicated action ownership were designed to improve resolution consistency and cut the rework caused by unclear QC feedback.
