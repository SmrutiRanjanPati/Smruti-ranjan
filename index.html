About

I design interfaces for problems that start as spreadsheets. Over the last three years I've worked on AI-driven dashboards, enterprise design systems and data-heavy tools - taking messy inputs and giving people something they can actually read at a glance.

I care about the parts of design that don't photograph well: information hierarchy, accessibility, and whether a component library still makes sense a year after launch. I also still write front-end code, which keeps my designs honest about what's buildable.
